      Trial clock

      The Fabric trial lasts 60 days, and Microsoft provisions it as an F4 or F64 capacity depending on your tenant. Resizing does not reset the clock, so start it on the first day of phase 1. On F4, run the heavier builds early. If the trial expires, non-Power BI items go inactive but stay in OneLake for 7 days, so export any PBIX you want to keep.

      Habits that pay off

      Small rules with a large effect.

      • Use synthetic or public sample data only. Never client or patient data. AdventureWorks, Mockaroo and Synthea are safe choices.
      • Tear down each build workspace before starting the next to free capacity.
      • Write one line per session in a LOG.md file in your repo.
      • End every phase with something you can show, not just something you read.

      A weekly rhythm

      About 9 hours: three short weekday blocks of 1.5 hours and one longer build session. Move the days to suit you.
